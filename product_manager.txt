Specialist Product Management Recruitment

Product management sits at the centre of every successful technology business, connecting customer needs, commercial strategy and engineering execution. A specialist product recruitment partner focuses on identifying individuals who can define vision, prioritise effectively and deliver products that drive measurable impact.

We operate across the full product lifecycle — from early-stage discovery and product-market fit through to scaling mature product organisations. Our clients range from venture-backed startups and scale-ups to global SaaS platforms and enterprise technology businesses.

What differentiates a true specialist in this space is a deep understanding of how product functions operate, how they interact with engineering and go-to-market teams, and how great product leaders balance customer insight with commercial outcomes.

Executive Product Leadership

At the most senior level, we support the appointment of product leaders responsible for defining company-wide product strategy and aligning it with business objectives.

Key roles include:

Chief Product Officer (CPO)
Chief Digital Officer (CDO)
Chief Experience Officer (CXO)
Chief Product & Technology Officer (CPTO)
VP Product / VP Product Management
VP Product Strategy / VP Growth Product
VP Product Design
These leaders are responsible for setting product vision, building and scaling product organisations, defining operating models and working closely with the executive team to drive growth and innovation.

We also support board and non-executive appointments where deep product expertise is required.

Product Leadership & Strategy

Below executive level, we work extensively with senior product leaders responsible for translating vision into execution.

This includes:

Product Directors
Heads of Product
Group Product Managers
Principal Product Managers
Lead Product Managers
Heads of Product Strategy
Heads of Product Operations
Heads of Growth Product
Heads of Data or AI Product
These individuals typically own product portfolios, manage teams of product managers and work cross-functionally with engineering, design and commercial teams. They are accountable for roadmap definition, prioritisation, product performance and long-term value creation.

Core Product Management

At the core of the function are product managers responsible for day-to-day product development, delivery and iteration.

We recruit across:

Product Managers
Senior Product Managers
Technical Product Managers
Platform Product Managers
Digital Product Managers
Growth Product Managers
Data and AI Product Managers
These roles involve defining requirements, working closely with engineering teams, managing backlogs and ensuring that products are aligned with both user needs and business goals.

Technical product managers, in particular, play a key role in infrastructure-heavy or API-driven environments, where deeper engineering understanding is required.

Product Ownership & Agile Delivery

In many organisations, particularly those operating in agile environments, product ownership is a distinct discipline focused on delivery execution.

We recruit:

Product Owners
Scrum Product Owners
Agile Product Specialists
These individuals are responsible for managing backlogs, prioritising features, working closely with engineering teams and ensuring efficient sprint delivery.

Product Operations & Enablement

As product organisations scale, product operations becomes increasingly important in driving efficiency, consistency and performance.

We specialise in:

Product Operations Managers
Product Operations Directors
Product Analysts
Product Enablement Specialists
These roles focus on improving product processes, implementing tooling, supporting data-driven decision making and enabling product teams to operate at scale.

Product Design, UX & Research

Product management is closely linked with design and user experience. We recruit talent that ensures products are intuitive, user-centric and aligned with real customer needs.

Key roles include:

UX Designers
Product Designers
UX Researchers
Service Designers
Interaction Designers
These professionals are responsible for user research, journey mapping, prototyping and ensuring that product decisions are grounded in real user insight.

Growth, Experimentation & Data-Driven Product

Modern product organisations increasingly rely on data and experimentation to drive growth and optimisation.

We recruit across:

Growth Product Managers
Experimentation Leads
Product Analysts
Data Product Managers
These individuals focus on metrics, A/B testing, user behaviour analysis and continuous optimisation of product performance, particularly in SaaS, marketplace and consumer applications.

Platform, Data & AI Product

A highly specialised and fast-growing area within product management is platform, data and AI-focused product roles.

We recruit:

Platform Product Managers
API Product Managers
Data Product Managers
AI / Machine Learning Product Managers
These roles require a deeper technical understanding and are typically responsible for internal platforms, developer tools, data infrastructure or AI-driven features.

They are particularly critical in:

Data platforms and analytics companies
AI-first businesses
Developer tooling and infrastructure organisations

Conclusion

Product management is the discipline that ensures technology delivers value — connecting what is possible from an engineering perspective with what is needed from a customer and commercial standpoint.

A specialist product recruitment partner understands this balance. By covering the full spectrum of product roles — from executive leadership through to hands-on product managers and designers — and by maintaining deep knowledge of product environments and methodologies, we help organisations build product teams that can define, deliver and scale successful technology products.
